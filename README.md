Generative AI Foundations

Day 1 introduced the fundamentals of Generative AI and its applications in aviation management. The lab covered the difference between Traditional AI and Generative AI, with aviation examples such as flight-delay prediction and generating passenger delay notifications.

The lab also compared ChatGPT and Gemini for aviation-business tasks and explored how Generative AI can support customer service, marketing, operations, human resources and passenger experience.

Other topics included retrieved information versus generated information, AI capabilities such as prediction, classification, recommendation and generation, and different GenAI output types including text, images, audio, video, code and presentations.

The lab also introduced the concept of a foundation model and showed how it can be adapted for different aviation-related tasks. The key learning was that GenAI can support multiple aviation-business functions, but human review is important for checking accuracy, privacy and suitability before professional use.
